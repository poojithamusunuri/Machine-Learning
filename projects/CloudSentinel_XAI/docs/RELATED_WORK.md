# Related work starting points
- CSE-CIC-IDS2018: https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2018.html
- AWS registry: https://registry.opendata.aws/cse-cic-ids2018/
- Dai et al. (2024), PLOS ONE: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0308469
- Consensus hybrid ensemble learning for IDS with XAI (2024): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnca.2024.104091
- Evaluating Explainable Hybrid IDS Under Zero-Day Conditions (2026): https://doi.org/10.3390/ai7090346

Do not claim the broad combination of hybrid ML and XAI is unprecedented. Investigate contribution through multiple held-out families, false-alarm operating points, explanation stability/fidelity, and reproducibility.
